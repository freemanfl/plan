# Web Studio Reference & Competitor Benchmarks

## Purpose

This file stores external businesses/sites used as evidence, benchmarks, pricing references, design references, or sources of ideas.

Keep separate distinctions between:
- business-model reference;
- sales/funnel reference;
- visual-quality reference;
- price-to-quality benchmark;
- technical/service benchmark.

---

## 1. Oak Harbor Web Designs

### Role
Primary business-model reference.

Useful ideas:
- recurring website service;
- strong "never worry about your website" positioning;
- direct founder responsibility;
- design/build/hosting/edits/support bundled;
- small-business focus;
- hand-coded/static positioning;
- performance and proof;
- clear pricing and risk reduction.

Main lesson:
**The deeper product is responsibility and peace of mind, not merely files.**

Oak Harbor is also an important funnel reference for trust, service, recurring support, and small-business positioning.

## 4. Build 76

### Role
Very close UK analogue and strong sales/service reference.

Useful ideas:
- "You run the business. We run the website" positioning;
- free homepage prototype before payment;
- founder-led trust;
- managed website responsibility;
- hosting/domain/email/security/backups/changes;
- local SEO / lead capture / integrations on stronger offers;
- automation around Google Business/reviews/content/reporting.

Important difference:
Build 76 uses tiers where higher prices can include more support/complexity. Our preferred direction is **one offer** and no deliberate sale of substantially more founder time.

Strong idea worth testing:
**prototype first, pay after client sees direction.**

## 7. Pole Studio / Allure Studio

URL: https://pole-studio.eu/

### Role
**Price-to-quality benchmark only for now. Not a template/design target.**

Known real-world price from owner/friend context:
**roughly 12–15k CZK.**

Assessment:
- visually decent but not at Fraser/IHI quality;
- complete and functional;
- multilingual/business content/booking/gallery/team/pricing make it commercially useful;
- likely around mid-level visual quality but strong value for the price.

Main lesson:
At ~12–15k CZK, the local market can deliver a complete respectable site even when visual art direction is only moderate.

This gives us a useful commercial floor:
**If our system can produce visibly stronger design while maintaining similar functional completeness, the product is credible.**
