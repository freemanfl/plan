# Maintenance

Production builds the site. Maintenance looks after it once it is live. They are separate loops.

The offer includes remaining responsible for ordinary changes so the client does not run hosting, code, or plugins themselves. That is part of what is being sold, not an extra product.

Typical work is business-driven: hours, prices, photos, copy, contact details, a normal new piece of content. It is not a second design project and not unlimited restyling.

How requests arrive, who does the work, what is in or out of scope, and how updates go live are not defined yet.

See [README.md](README.md) for the idea and [production-pipeline.md](production-pipeline.md) for how a site is made.
